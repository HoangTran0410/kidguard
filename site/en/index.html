01How it works

Two devices, nothing in between

KidGuard becomes the phone's Device Owner, the Android role a company uses for work phones. That is what lets it lock apps, keep location on and stop itself from being removed, with no root.

Child's phone KidGuard · Device Owner

Keeps the rules and applies them itself, even offline. Serves the parent page on port 8770.

  • Daily limit, bedtime, school hours
  • Lock apps, approve new ones
  • Location, SOS, "more time" button
At home: Wi-FiDirect, instant, through no one
Away: optionalTailscale, or a Cloudflare Worker you run
Parents A browser, or the parent app

Safari, Chrome, laptop or phone, iPhone included. Pair once with a 6-digit code, protected by a PIN.

02Features

Enough for everyday use, nothing padded

Everything below is in the current release. No paid tier, no "coming soon".

Time

Daily limit
One number, or one per weekday. When time is up every app locks until midnight, except "Always allowed" ones. Add 15 / 30 / 60 minutes for today when needed.
Bedtime, school hours
Bedtime every day, plus windows on chosen days (school hours Monday to Friday), across midnight too. The child gets a heads-up 5 minutes and 1 minute before.
Pause now
30 minutes, 1 hour, or until 6 am.
Ask for more time
The child asks for 15 / 30 / 60 minutes with a reason, or picks a chore you listed ("Wash the dishes, +20 min"). You approve or decline, optionally with a note.
Rule templates
School day, Weekend, Summer holiday, Exams, or save your own. Apply to one kid or several at once.

Apps

Per app
Normal, N minutes a day, Block, or Always allowed. Select many to change them at once. A locked app's icon turns grey and Android says the parent paused it.
New apps
Optionally locked until you approve them.
Lighter phone
Hide the maker's preinstalled apps the child doesn't use (they stop running and updating; untick to bring back), see the heaviest apps, clear an app's data. No fake "RAM cleaner".
Updates itself
Once a day the phone checks GitHub for a new release, downloads it, verifies the SHA-256 and installs it. Can be turned off in Settings.

Looking back

History
Screen time for 7 days, top apps per day, a weekly report versus the week before. Several kids: an overview screen, one card per kid, one shared map.
The child's rules
The child can see their own rules on the phone, and a log of what the parents changed. Transparency is a feature, not a bug.

Location and safety

Location
Locate now, 7-day history with stops on an OpenStreetMap map. The child cannot turn location off.
Zones
Home, School... with a radius. You are told when the child arrives or leaves.
SOS
A red button on the child's phone. The parent page shows the time and a fresh location; the parent app notifies in seconds (through the Worker and Firebase) or within 15 minutes.
See and control the screen
The child's screen live (about 3 frames per second), tap, swipe, type. Needs Android 11+. The child always sees "Your parent is viewing the screen".
Listen around
At most 10 minutes at a time, home Wi-Fi or Tailscale only. The phone beeps, shows a notification and Android's mic dot; this cannot be turned off.
Alerts
Arrived at or left a zone, battery at 15 %, a new app installed, the clock changed.

Holding firm

Tamper-proof
Blocks uninstall, force stop, factory reset from Settings, Safe Mode, new users, clock changes, airplane mode. Restarts itself if killed. Optional: family DNS filter (AdGuard, Cloudflare, CleanBrowsing), block app installs, block USB debugging.
Commands
Lock the screen, ring, full-screen message, restart the phone.
Undo everything
Parent page, Settings, Gỡ quyền quản lý (remove management): every app unlocked, every restriction lifted, then uninstall KidGuard like any app.
The parent page is in Vietnamese for now. Buttons named on this page are given as they appear in the app. The layout is simple enough to follow with a browser translator.

05Install

Pick the way that matches what you have

Android only lets an app become Device Owner while the phone has no accounts (Google, Samsung, Xiaomi...). Remove them first, install, then sign back in: everything comes back. Only Basic mode (E) skips this step.

A
Child's phone on Android 11+, Wi-Fi

On the phone itself Recommended

  1. Remove the accounts on the child's phone. Install the APK, open KidGuard.
  2. Tap Cài đầy đủ không cần máy tính (Android 11+) (full setup without a computer).
  3. Turn on Wireless debugging in Developer options, choose Pair with code, type the 6 digits into KidGuard's notification. It does the rest and turns wireless debugging off again.
B
A new or factory-reset phone

Scan a QR code

  1. On the welcome screen, tap 6 times on an empty spot.
  2. Scan this QR.
  3. The phone downloads KidGuard over Wi-Fi and sets it up. Open it and finish the short checklist.

Some skins drop the 6-tap screen (Oppo and Realme ColorOS on Android 8–9): use way C.

C
A computer and a USB cable

Installer page in Chrome or Edge

  1. Turn on USB debugging on the child's phone, plug it into the computer.
  2. Open the installer page, press Connect. No adb needed.
D
Comfortable with a terminal

Script on macOS, Linux, Windows

scripts/setup.sh (downloads adb if missing) or scripts\setup.ps1 from the source repository.

E
Any Android 8+, no computer

Basic mode, no Device Owner

  1. Install the APK, open KidGuard, tap Cài nhanh không cần máy tính (quick setup without a computer).
  2. Turn on each item in the list: device admin, accessibility, usage access, location, battery. Accounts can stay.

Weaker than Device Owner. Blocked apps are closed back to the home screen instead of greyed out. Not available: DNS filter, blocking installs, locking airplane mode or the clock, reboot, Tailscale. A child who boots into Safe Mode or turns accessibility off stops the blocking (the parent page shows a red warning and the parent app notifies). Switch to Device Owner later with ways A to D.

After installing

The child's phone shows a 6-digit code and a QR code. On your phone, same Wi-Fi, scan the QR with the camera (it opens the page with the code filled in), or open http://<phone IP>:8770 and type the code. In Cài đặt (Settings) set a parent PIN: without it the phone refuses to pair another browser.

Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Samsung

On the child's phone tap Cài đặt pin / tự khởi động (battery / autostart) and let KidGuard run in the background, or the maker's battery saver may kill it.

Blocked by Google Play Protect ("App blocked to protect your device")? Play Protect blocks apps installed from a browser or file manager when they use accessibility (Basic mode needs it). On the child's phone: Play Store, your avatar, Play Protect, the gear icon, turn off Scan apps with Play Protect. Install and set up KidGuard, then turn it back on. QR setup is not affected, since the phone downloads the app itself.
06Away from home

Still in charge when you are out

At home the parent page connects straight over Wi-Fi. Away, pick one of two ways, or use both. Both are free.

Tailscale Recommended

A private network between the child's phone and yours. Instant, nothing to deploy.

  1. Make a free account at tailscale.com, generate an auth key: Reusable on, Ephemeral off.
  2. Parent page (on home Wi-Fi): Cài đặt, Điều khiển từ xa qua Tailscale (Settings, remote control via Tailscale), paste the key, press Bật (On). The phone downloads Tailscale, joins your network and hides the app, about a minute.
  3. Install Tailscale on your phone, log in with the same account, open http://100.x.y.z:8770.

The child cannot turn the VPN off or uninstall Tailscale, and only sees a key icon in the status bar. The key stays on the child's phone, never goes through the Worker.

Cloudflare Worker Advanced

A small Worker of your own, free plan, relaying commands, state and the live screen. Frames stay in memory only, never stored. Opens in any browser.

  1. Deploy the Worker (needs Node): cd worker && npm install && npx wrangler login && npx wrangler deploy
  2. Parent page (on home Wi-Fi): Cài đặt, Nâng cao: relay Cloudflare Worker (Settings, advanced: relay), paste the Worker address, press On.
  3. Open the link it shows on your phone.

07FAQ

Good to know before you install

Can the child uninstall KidGuard?

Not from Settings. A factory reset from the hardware keys (recovery mode) still works: KidGuard is then gone and the parent page shows "disconnected". In Basic mode, Safe Mode also stops the blocking, so use Device Owner when you can.

Does it need root?

No. KidGuard uses Android's built-in Device Owner role, the one companies use for work phones. No root, no system modifications.

Does it work on older Android?

Android 8 and up. Live screen view and remote control need Android 11+, and so does the on-the-phone install. On Android 8 to 10 the child can force stop KidGuard (it starts again after a reboot).

Is any data sent anywhere?

Not by default: the parent page is served by your child's own phone over home Wi-Fi. Only if you turn on the Cloudflare Worker do commands and state go through your own Worker. No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Details: PRIVACY.md.

Does the child know they are being managed?

Yes, on purpose. The child can see their own rules on the phone (Luật của con) and a log of what the parents changed. Screen viewing shows a notice; listening always beeps and shows a notification. KidGuard is only for a parent managing their own minor child's phone; installing it on an adult's phone is covert surveillance and illegal in most countries.

Can the DNS filter cut off my child's Internet?

No. If the filter's server fails or the network blocks it for 90 seconds, KidGuard falls back to normal DNS for 30 minutes and the parent page says why.

Why do some apps show a black screen?

Apps that mark their screen as secure (banking and similar) appear black when viewed remotely.

Is it secure?

Each browser pairs once with a 6-digit code shown on the child's phone, and pairing more needs the parent PIN. The PIN is stored as a salted hash and never sent back to the page. Note: on home Wi-Fi the page runs over plain HTTP, so only use it on a network you trust.

Found a bug or have an idea?

Send it to GitHub Issues. How the parts talk to each other is described in docs/protocol.md.
