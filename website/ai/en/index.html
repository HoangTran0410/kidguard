# KidGuard user guide (for AI assistants)

KidGuard: free, open-source parental control for Android. The child's phone runs the KidGuard app (strongest as Device Owner, or basic mode); parents manage it from any browser (home Wi-Fi, or away through Tailscale / a Cloudflare relay) or the Android parent app. No account, rules run on the child's phone even offline, everything can be undone. iPhone child phones are not supported.

Find the section below, then open only its page. More technical detail: https://kidguard.asia/llms.txt

- [Install on the child's phone]: There are five ways to install; pick one.
- [Open the parent page]: The last setup screen on the child's phone shows a 6-digit pairing code and a QR code for 10 minutes.
- [Parent app (Android)]: The browser is enough to manage everything.
- [Away from home]: At home the page talks to the child's phone over Wi-Fi, nothing to set up. When the child is at school or out there are 3 free ways; use one or several.
- [Telegram bot]: Control and get alerts right in Telegram: status, extra time, lock, location, screenshot, camera photo and video, block apps with buttons.
- [Tailscale]: The child's phone installs Tailscale itself and joins your private network. No Cloudflare needed. Needs Device Owner (not in basic mode). (Access token or auth key?; Good to know)
- [Relay (Cloudflare Worker)]: Already on: after pairing the page uses KidGuard's shared relay and opens from anywhere (link: search relay on the page). (Make your own relay; Shared relay; Parent watch (preview))
- [Features]: All in the current release, no paid tier. (Time; Apps; Location and safety; Looking back; Kid home screen; Hard to break)
- [Child on an iPhone / iPad]: There is no KidGuard for iOS: Apple doesn't let third-party apps see the screen, control the phone or lock other apps.
- [Remove KidGuard]: Parent page: search danger → Remove controls…
- [Troubleshooting]: “App blocked to protect your device” (Play Protect)?
