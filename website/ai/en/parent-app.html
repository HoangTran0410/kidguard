# Parent app (Android)

The browser is enough to manage everything. The parent app adds **notifications** (SOS, more-time requests, accessibility turned off, lost connection) and shows the last data when the child's phone is offline.

1. [Download the parent app](https://github.com/HoangTran0410/kidguard/releases/latest/download/kidguard-parent.apk) and install it on your phone.
2. Open it and tap **Add kid's phone** .
3. At home: type the address shown on the child's KidGuard screen, then the 6-digit pairing code (hold **Parents: show the pairing code** for 5 s if you see none).
4. Away from home: tap **Scan QR** and scan the code on the child phone. Only KidGuard links are accepted.
5. The other parent: on your page search `relay` and tap **Invite another parent** . They scan the QR (or open the link) and type a name. The invite works once and ends after 10 minutes.
6. Each parent has their own access: that section lists who has it, with rename and remove. Commands and rules show the sender's name in the child's log. A browser asks the family PIN again every 12 hours.
7. Two kids or more: **Overview** shows every kid on one page.

**Easier:** the [Telegram bot] delivers SOS and more-time requests at once, no app or build needed.

A time request notification has **+15 / +30 min** and **Decline** buttons right on it. The app checks about every 15 minutes. For SOS in seconds (through the Worker and Firebase) you build the app yourself: see the [README](https://github.com/HoangTran0410/kidguard#instant-sos-notifications-optional).

**Notifications check:** the app has a health card (**Settings**) that says if alerts can reach you (permission, channels, battery saver, last check) and fixes each in one tap. Offline phones show &ldquo;offline since HH:mm&rdquo;.

Parent on an iPhone: there is no app, the browser (Safari) is enough.
