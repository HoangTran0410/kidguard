# Open the parent page

The last setup screen on the child's phone shows a **6-digit pairing code** and a **QR code** for 10 minutes. Later: hold **Parents: show the pairing code** for 5 seconds on the KidGuard screen.

1. On your phone (same Wi-Fi as the child's), scan the QR with the camera. The page opens with the code filled in.
2. Or open a browser at `http://<child phone IP>:8770` and type the 6-digit pairing code.
3. Set a **parent PIN** in the pairing form. Without it the child's phone refuses to pair another browser.
4. Sign back in to the accounts on the child's phone (if you removed them) and unplug the cable.
5. Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Samsung: on the child's phone open **Battery and autostart** and let KidGuard run in the background.
6. To manage when your child is out too: see [Away from home] .

> **Oppo / Realme / OnePlus (ColorOS):** open Recents and pull the KidGuard card down to lock it (padlock icon). In Phone Manager allow KidGuard to autostart and run in the background. Swiping KidGuard away in Recents on ColorOS stops the app completely.

> **Finding a setting:** on the parent page, tap **Find a setting** at the top (or press `/`) and type a few letters, like `relay`, `bedtime`, `wifi`. This guide says “search `…`” instead of a menu path, since menus can move.

**First time: 4 quick steps.** Right after pairing the page walks you through **starting level** (Light, Balanced or Strict), your **top apps** (allow, normal or block), **bedtime and daily limit**, and **what the kid sees**. Nothing is saved until **Done**. Redo it any time: search `set up again`.

On home Wi-Fi the page is plain HTTP: use it only on networks you trust. Each browser pairs once with the 6-digit code; pairing more needs the parent PIN.
