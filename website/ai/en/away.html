# Away from home

At home the page talks to the child's phone over Wi-Fi, nothing to set up. When the child is at school or out there are 3 free ways; use one or several. **The shared relay is already on** from pairing, so the page opens from anywhere even if you do nothing.

- **Just alerts and quick taps** (extra time, lock, location): the [Telegram bot] .
- **Everything like at home** (live screen, listen around): [Tailscale] .
- **Only a browser** : the [relay] (already on; make your own if you don't want data going through the author's server).

|  | Telegram bot | Tailscale | Relay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Setup | ~2 min: make a bot, paste its token | ~5 min, once: Tailscale app on the parent's phone, paste one key | None (shared relay is on). Your own: ~3 min on a computer |
| Used with | The Telegram app, buttons | The parent page (Tailscale app on) | The parent page, any browser |
| Speed | ~1 second | Instant | Usually a few seconds (if the live link drops: ~20 s, screen off ~60 s) |
| Screen / listen | Screenshot, 10–60 s recording | Live screen, live listen | Live screen, no listen |
| SOS, more-time requests | Arrive at once in Telegram | In the page / parent app | In the page / parent app |
| Data goes through | Telegram's servers | Nobody (end-to-end encrypted) | Shared: the author's Cloudflare. Your own: only your account |
| Needs | Internet on the child's phone. One bot per child | Device Owner (not in basic mode) | Nothing |
| Child sees | Nothing (a notice while recording) | A key icon in the status bar (VPN) | Nothing |

The Telegram bot, Tailscale and your own relay are all **turned on while at home**, in the parent page: search `telegram`, search `tailscale` or search `relay`.
