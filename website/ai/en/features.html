# Features

All in the current release, no paid tier.

## Time

Daily limit

One number, or one per weekday. When time is up every app (except “Always allowed”) locks until midnight. +15 / +30 / +60 min for today.

Bedtime, schedules

Bedtime every day, plus windows on chosen days (school hours Mon–Fri); can cross midnight.

Heads-up before locking

The child gets a notification 5 min and 1 min before the limit, bedtime or a schedule locks the phone.

More time always works

Approve a request during bedtime or school hours: the phone opens for N minutes, then locks again. Tap **End** to lock early.

Bedtime per night, +1 min

A separate bedtime for each night (school nights differ from weekends). When it locks, the child can take **+1 minute** once per lock to save their work. Can be turned off.

Pause now

30 min, 1 h, or until 6 am.

Ask for more time

15 / 30 / 60 min plus a reason; the parent approves or declines.

Chores

The parent lists chores (“Wash the dishes +20 min”); the child picks one when asking. Approve with a note if you like.

Unlock code

6 digits from Google Authenticator, no network on either phone. The child gets 15–120 min free of limits; each code works once.

Rule templates

School day, Weekend, Summer holiday, Exams, or save your own. Apply to one kid or several.

## Apps

Per app

Normal, N minutes a day, Block, or Always allowed. Select many to change them at once.

App groups

One shared daily limit for a group (“Games”, “Social”) and/or hours when it may be used.

New apps

Optionally locked until a parent approves them.

Lighter phone

Hide the maker's preinstalled apps the child doesn't use, see the heaviest apps, clear an app's data. Needs Device Owner.

Updates itself

Once a day the child's phone checks GitHub releases, downloads the new APK and checks its SHA-256. Off or update now in Settings. Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO (HyperOS): the phone asks you to confirm each time, see [Troubleshooting].

## Location and safety

Location

Locate now, location forced on (the child can't turn it off), 7-day history on an OpenStreetMap map.

Zones

Home, School… with a radius: you're told when the child arrives or leaves.

SOS

A red button on the child's phone: the parent page shows the time and a fresh location; the parent app notifies.

See the screen

The child's screen live (Android 11+, ~3 fps). **Save picture** keeps one frame, **Record** makes a video (saved on the parent's device; stops when tapped again or when viewing stops). The child is told the parent is viewing.

Remote control

Tap, swipe, long press, Back / Home / Recents, type text.

Listen around

At most 10 min at a time, over home Wi-Fi, Tailscale or the relay. The child always knows: a beep, a notification, Android's mic dot.

Lost mode

**Where** tab: turning it on locks the phone at once and again on every unlock, shows your message + number on the lock screen, locates every 5 min and rings 5 min in every 15. Turn it off and everything goes back. Also alerts on a SIM change.

Alert levels

Only SOS, lost, tamper and requests make noise; the rest arrive quietly. Telegram: `/quiet` picks all / important / SOS only per person. The parent app says “offline since HH:mm” and skips the child's bedtime.

Alerts

Arrived / left a zone, battery at 15 %, a new app installed, the clock changed.

## Looking back

Notifications, SMS, word alerts

Turn each one on in **What you can see**: read your kid's notifications and text messages, and get an alert when a word of yours shows up. Each is off until you turn it on. Your kid is always told.

Camera on request

Turn it on in **What you can see**. Tap **Take photo**, or **Watch live** to see the camera for up to 10 minutes (one way, sound optional; the round button records it to a video on your device). Not on yet? The page asks: tap **Turn on and continue** (photo) or **Turn on camera** (live). Your kid sees a notice the whole time. Photos you opened stay viewable when the phone is offline.

History

Screen time for 7 days, top apps per day, a weekly report per kid.

All kids

**Overview**: a card per kid, every kid's location on one map, 7 days side by side.

Child's rules

The child sees their own rules and a log of what the parents changed.

Languages

English and Vietnamese in the parent page and both apps.

## Kid home screen

Gestures

Hold an icon: a menu over a dimmed screen, with **Edit Home Screen**. Drag an app onto another to make a folder. In edit mode, the minus badge removes (it asks first); pick several to group or remove at once.

Folders, library

Folders open from their icon; hold to re-arrange. **App Library** on the last page: suggestions, recently added, categories, search.

Widgets

Clock, month, time left, timetable (morning/afternoon, bell times, whole week), chores, notes, battery, weather, lock screen; other apps' widgets too.

&ldquo;Today&rdquo; page

Swipe right from page 1. SOS and Call always sit on top. Cards: **Right now** (class now, time until bedtime), **Screen time** (ask for more), **Tomorrow** (from 5 pm), chores, notes, **Files with parents** (the kid can send a file from here). **Choose cards** at the end; turn the whole page off in Customize.

This week

&ldquo;Your week&rdquo; card on Today: days within the daily limit, the current run, finished lists. It hides when there is nothing to celebrate.

Requests

One open request per kid: the child sees **waiting** and can **Remind**, then the last answer. The child can also ask to open a blocked or new app. You answer with **N/2**, **N** or **2N** minutes, on the page, in the parent app or in Telegram (`/requests`); once answered it updates everywhere. Unanswered requests expire at midnight.

Customize

Hold an empty spot on the home screen: **Widgets**, **Edit Home Screen**, **Customize**. In Customize: wallpaper (blur, dim), columns, icon size (rows stay the same), icon shape (circle, square, squircle), label size, font, light/dark, accent colour, notification dots, effects Full / Light / Auto. **Undo** appears right under what you changed.

Saved layouts

**Save current layout** (up to 5, named): pages, dock, look and photo wallpaper. Tap one to switch the whole home, with undo. Stored on the phone only.

Control center

Swipe down on the right half of the home screen: Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, airplane, data, torch, rotation, Do Not Disturb, brightness, volume, now playing, **Study**. The left half opens notifications.

Study mode

The kid starts it: only study apps stay on the home screen, Do Not Disturb on if allowed. Apps the parent blocked stay blocked.

Hidden apps

In the App Library, ⋮ next to search: every hidden app with the reason (blocked, waiting for OK, study time...). A page KidGuard closes always says why in a short message.

No parental control?

This launcher has its own app: [KidGuard Home](https://github.com/HoangTran0410/kidguard/releases/latest/download/kidguard-home.apk) (under 1 MB). Fits any phone, needs no parent and no special permissions; SOS, time requests, chores and files are left out. Open it and tap **Fix** on the banner to make it the home screen.

Already have KidGuard?

No need for KidGuard Home: the launcher is built in. Install KidGuard later and the layout, notes, timetable and wallpaper move over by themselves (KidGuard Home 0.9.10 or newer); other apps' widgets must be added again.

Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO

With a home screen that is not Xiaomi's, navigation turns into 3 buttons: the system does this and no setting keeps gestures. Pick Xiaomi's home screen again to get gestures back.

## Hard to break

Tamper-proof

Blocks uninstall, force stop, factory reset from Settings, Safe Mode, new users, changing the clock, airplane mode. A watchdog restarts it.

Factory reset protection

Device Owner, Android 11+. After a reset from recovery the phone asks for the parent's Google account. Needs that account's **numeric ID** (not the email); the page shows how to get it. **A wrong ID can lock the phone for good after a reset.** Turned off when you remove controls.

PIN tries left

A wrong PIN on the child's phone says how many tries are left (5 at first). After that it locks for 5 minutes, then longer, up to 30. **Forgot PIN?** shows how to reset (24 h wait, with an alert).

Command receipts

Every command shows **sent**, then **ran HH:mm**, **refused** or **expired** (with **Resend**). A wrong PIN over the relay is not remembered.

Optional

Family DNS filter (AdGuard / Cloudflare / CleanBrowsing), block installs, block USB debugging.

Commands

Lock the screen, ring, full-screen message, restart the phone.

Locked apps use Android's own mechanism: the icon turns grey and opening it says the parent paused it.
